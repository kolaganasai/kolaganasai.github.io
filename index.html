UPI Scam Shield

Flags scam SMS written in English, Hinglish and Hindi and explains why, fully offline on an Android phone.

  • Combines TF-IDF word and character features with 20 fraud rules, like “asks for your UPI PIN to receive money”.
  • Trained on a synthetic dataset covering 11 scam types, with genuine bank alerts and OTPs so normal messages aren't flagged.
  • Runs as a 500 KB model inside a Kotlin Android app and explains each alert in English or Hindi.
